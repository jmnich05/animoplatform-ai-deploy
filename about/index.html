About Ánimo

Agency-grade work from someone who has run the store.

“The best system is not the most impressive one. It is the one people can use, understand and keep improving.”

Ánimo is a boutique growth studio for e-commerce brands and therapy practices, founded by Jonathan Nichols in Louisville, Kentucky. Jon has spent 10+ years running e-commerce in-house: the store, merchandising, the ads, the email and SMS, and the reports the owners actually read.

That range matters because growth problems rarely stay inside one channel. A checkout issue can be a merchandising issue. A slow month can be a search problem. A reporting gap can hide which ads are working. Ánimo works on the whole path, then shows you what changed.

Who Ánimo works with

Two kinds of clients. Both run on trust.

Ánimo’s current clients include two Louisville practices, Acadia Counseling and Closer Horizons. The same craft serves independent stores that need to earn a stranger’s trust online.
