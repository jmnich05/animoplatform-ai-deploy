About Ánimo

Consulting from someone who lives inside the work.

“The best system is not the most impressive one. It is the one people can use, understand and keep improving.”

Ánimo is a Louisville consulting practice founded by Jonathan Nichols. Jon’s operating background spans e-commerce, merchandising, marketing technology, analytics, customer experience, fulfillment and AI-assisted workflows.

That range matters because digital problems rarely stay inside one department. A checkout issue can be a merchandising issue. A reporting problem can be a process problem. A request for “an AI agent” can really be a calendar, inbox or ownership problem.

Why the sprout

Growth is not a launch. It is a living system.

The Ánimo mark starts with a sprout because useful change begins small: one clearer page, one smoother workflow, one better daily habit. The work grows through iteration, context and care.
