For small businesses · Shops, studios, clinics and service firms

The work, step by step, on one small business.

Tanager Auto Works is a Louisville auto shop we made up, so we can show every step without sharing a client’s numbers. Four steps, in the order we’d do them: the searches worth winning, the pages that win them, a site that books the job, and ads that bring in more.

1 Organic keywords

Find the searches worth winning.

Most shops chase “auto repair” and lose to the chains. We start with what people nearby actually type, and pick the searches a good local shop can win: a specific job, a place, and someone ready to book.

  • Pull every search the site already shows up for, and where it ranks.
  • Add the jobs the shop wants more of: brakes, AC, diagnostics.
  • Rank them by demand, by distance from page one, and by what the job is worth.

For Tanager: 38 searches chosen. Nine are within reach in the first 90 days.

2 SEO pages

Give each search a page that deserves to rank.

One “Services” page can’t answer forty different questions. Each job gets its own page that says what it is, what it costs, how long it takes and how to book, in plain words that Google and AI tools can read.

  • A page for each service and each neighborhood the shop serves.
  • Price ranges, timing and common questions answered up front.
  • Structured data, so Google can show hours, prices and reviews.
  • A Google Business Profile that matches the site, word for word.

For Tanager: twelve service and neighborhood pages in two months. The brake page climbs from #14 to #3 in about 90 days.

3 Site and booking

Make booking take a minute.

Ranking only pays when the visit turns into a job. We cut the path from landing to booked down to three taps, show prices and openings up front, and let people book at 9 at night, when the shop is closed.

  • The answer first: price range, time needed, next opening.
  • Real-time booking tied to the shop’s own calendar.
  • Tap to call or text from every page.
  • Pages that load in about a second on a phone.

For Tanager: booking goes from a phone call during shop hours to three taps at any hour. Try it on the phone.

4 Paid ads

Then pay for attention, and only the kind that books.

With pages that convert, ads stop being a gamble. Each ad lands on the page that answers it, and every call and booking is traced back to the ad that earned it.

Where AI ads stand today. Ads now appear inside AI answers. For a shop this size, that means Google’s AI results and Microsoft Copilot, which run from the same ad accounts as search ads. ChatGPT began showing ads in 2026, built for bigger budgets so far. We keep the shop’s pages and data ready for each one as it opens up.

For Tanager: ads start at about $900 a month and grow only on the campaigns that book jobs.
