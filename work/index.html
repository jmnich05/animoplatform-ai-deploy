Closer Horizons

A more useful path from first question to next step.

Closer Horizons is a Louisville psychology practice. Ánimo’s work focuses on making the digital experience clearer, calmer and easier to act on—without losing the warmth and trust the practice needs to convey.

Website experienceConsultation flowContent systemsMeasurement planning

Scope of work

From scattered decisions to one coherent experience.

  • Clarify the homepage story and the routes visitors actually need
  • Shape a direct, humane consultation path across desktop and mobile
  • Turn client input into practical page structure and implementation direction
  • Plan useful measurement around meaningful actions, not vanity clicks

The working principles

What the work is designed to protect.

01 · CLARITY

People know where they are.

Navigation and page structure reduce uncertainty instead of making visitors decode the organization.

02 · TRUST

The experience fits the service.

Language, pacing and interaction reflect the care required by the relationship—not generic funnel tactics.

03 · ACTION

The next step is obvious.

Calls, booking and supporting information work together so intent is not lost in friction.

04 · LEARNING

Measurement answers a question.

Tracking focuses on whether the new path helps people move forward, not on collecting more dashboards.
