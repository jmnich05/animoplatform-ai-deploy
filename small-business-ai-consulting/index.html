The real constraint

Your company probably has enough AI. It does not have enough usable context.

The knowledge agents need is usually fragmented across email, documents, spreadsheets, SaaS platforms, dashboards, and individual employees. Before adding more automation, we define what the data means, who owns it, who may access it, and which source should win when systems disagree.

Data consolidation & governance.

MCP servers & connectors.

Dashboards & insight.

Turn reliable inputs into decision-ready reporting, operational alerts, and plain-language analysis for the people who need to act.

Agents & workflows.

Build role-specific agents around recurring jobs, with clear authority, human checkpoints, and portable prompts and skills.

From source to return

One operating loop, not a pile of disconnected demos.

Audit the work and data.
