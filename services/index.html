What we fix

Four problems, and the work that solves each.

Start with the one that hurts most, or hand over all four. Either way, the person who scopes the work does the work.

01 Traffic that converts

More eyes, more bookings.

The problem
Not enough people find you online, and too few of the ones who do ever book, call or buy.
What we do
Build or rebuild the pages people land on, rank them in search, run Google and Meta ads to them, and track every booking, call and sale back to its source.

1.5–3×the traffic and conversions to expect in your first 12 months

Website · SEO · Google and Meta ads · Conversion tracking

02 Foot traffic

More people through your door.

The problem
Website sessions don’t pay the bills. Attention online has to turn into people at your location.
What we do
Tune your Google Business Profile and Maps listing, build location pages that rank for “near me” searches, run local ads, and count the calls and direction requests that follow.

Google Business Profile · Maps and local SEO · Local ads · Call and direction tracking

03 Reputation

A name people trust.

The problem
When someone looks you up, what they find is thin, out of date or different from one place to the next.
What we do
Make your name, hours, services and story match everywhere, set up a steady way to ask for reviews, and check what Google and AI tools say when someone asks about you.

Reviews · Listings · Brand and content · AI search visibility

04 What’s next

Ready for what’s next.

The problem
Most small-business sites were built for the way people searched five years ago.
What we do
Write plain answers to the questions customers ask, add the structured data search engines and AI tools read, and make booking and contact simple enough for a person or an AI agent to finish.

Structured data · Plain answers · Agent-ready booking and contact

The 1.5 to 3× range comes from the website sessions and conversions Ánimo clients have seen so far. It is what we plan for, not a guarantee: your starting point and your market set the pace.

How the work moves

Fix the site first. Then turn up the traffic.

Every search result, ad and referral lands on the same pages, so that is where the work starts.

01 · AUDIT

Find the leaks.

Speed, search, listings, ads and tracking, reviewed and ranked by what each fix is likely to be worth.

02 · FIX

Clear the path to booking.

Key pages, forms, calls and checkout first, because every visitor you earn or pay for lands there.

03 · GROW

Send in the right traffic.

SEO, local search, Google and Meta ads, and email and SMS, pointed at the pages that now convert.

04 · LEARN

Measure and refine.

Results land on your client board, so the next decision runs on customers, not platform noise.
